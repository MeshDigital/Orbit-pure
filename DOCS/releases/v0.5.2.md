## ORBIT 0.5.2

A mixing and crate-prep release. Auto-Mix now plays both tracks through one output at your sound card's own rate, Cue Forge lets you set the beat grid right on the drop, bar lines land on the "1" far more often, and Rekordbox writes keep playlists in sync.

> **Beta** (offered to the Beta and Any update channels only). The new Auto-Mix output path is new code that hasn't been played on many sound cards yet. If Auto-Mix sounds wrong or a transition clips, send a report with **Ctrl+Shift+F**: it attaches a screenshot and the recent log.

### Auto-Mix sound (#600, #631, #642, #636, #608, #612)
- **One output for both tracks:** both decks of an Auto-Mix play through a single mix bus, so the incoming track starts exactly on the beat instead of whenever a second audio device happens to start.
- **Your sound card's own sample rate:** the bus runs at the output device's native rate (44.1 / 48 / 96 kHz), so the device no longer restarts between tracks with different rates.
- **No clipping in the overlap:** a −1 dBFS safety limiter at the end of the mix catches peaks where both tracks are loud at once.
- **Fixes:** a crash when skipping between FLAC/M4A tracks mid-seek, an ASIO deck silently landing on the Windows default device, and half/double-time pairs (e.g. 87 and 174 BPM) now beat-match instead of echoing out.

### Cue Forge (#646, #634, #639, #633, #628, #627, #622, #620, #623, #625)
- **Set the grid on the drop:** click or drag in the bar-number ruler to put the cursor exactly on a bass hit without playing. **B** now moves the whole grid so a bar starts exactly there (one undo step); before, it only chose which existing beat was the "1".
- **Drops land where you put them:** **D** follows the Snap setting (beat, bar or off) instead of always jumping to the nearest bar, which could be up to 0.7 s away at 174 BPM. If the drop isn't on a bar line, Cue Forge says so and suggests pressing B there.
- **Drop rules:** choose where `[OUT]` lands after the drop (+16 / +32 / +48 / +64 bars or custom) and optionally add memory cues after it, from the DROP RULES bar under the waveform.
- **Drop 2:** found automatically when you place Drop 1 by hand, with a fallback to the energy curve when the phrase map misses it.
- **Faster crate prep:** keyboard shortcuts, hot loops on pads A–H (Serato loops are imported too), hold a cue or pad to preview it like on a CDJ, and a "Check these first" strip listing auto drops a second-opinion model disagrees with.
- Section suggestions stay hidden once a track has mix cues, and a live banner shows the incoming track during Auto-Mix crossfades.

### Beat grids (#638)
- **Bar lines on the "1":** a systematic one-beat-early bias in downbeat detection is fixed. On our test set, auto bar lines now match Rekordbox's on 43% of cues (was 24%) and hand-placed drops on 54% (was 43%). Applies to newly analysed tracks; grids you edited or confirmed are never changed.

### Rekordbox (#629, #635, #637)
- **Cleaner direct writes:** countdown cues stay on the memory strip so hot-cue pads A–H are free for jump points, and hot-cue memory duplicates are off by default.
- **Playlists stay in sync:** writing a playlist creates or updates it in Rekordbox with any missing tracks, and also updates `masterPlaylists6.xml` next to `master.db`. That file is now included in ORBIT's Rekordbox backups and restores.
- **Tracks Rekordbox doesn't have yet:** one click exports them to a `rekordbox.xml` you can import.

### Library
- **Export to USB / folder** (#641): right-click a playlist → Export → **Export to USB / folder…** copies its audio files and `PIONEER/rekordbox.xml` to a drive or folder. Progress now updates one toast instead of stacking one per file.
- **Faster large libraries** (#621): 30,000+ track libraries open their first page about 15× faster and scroll about 22× faster.
- **Navigation:** Analysis is back in the sidebar under LIBRARY & PREP (#624), and the Now Playing tab opens Now Playing again instead of the Workstation (#595).
- Legacy transition tables are removed after their data was verified in the unified Transitions store (#594).
