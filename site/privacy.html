Privacy Policy

Short version: ORBIT Pure runs entirely on your device. We do not operate servers, collect telemetry, track usage, or sell data.

1. Local-Only Operation

Your library database (SQLite), audio analysis results, cue points, waveforms, and configuration live strictly on your local computer. ORBIT Pure does not phone home with analytics or crash telemetry.

2. Credentials & Tokens

Soulseek credentials and optional Spotify/YouTube OAuth refresh tokens are stored locally using your operating system's secure credential storage (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, or Linux Secret Service / local encrypted storage) and are transmitted only to the respective service you explicitly connect to.

3. Direct Third-Party Connections

When you use ORBIT Pure, your device connects directly to:

  • The Soulseek peer-to-peer network (when you sign in and search/download).
  • Optional metadata or playlist services you explicitly enable (Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, MusicBrainz, Deezer, Discogs, AcoustID).
  • GitHub Releases when checking for application updates.

4. Open Source Verification

ORBIT Pure is open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0. You can audit the complete source code at github.com/MeshDigital/Orbit-pure.
